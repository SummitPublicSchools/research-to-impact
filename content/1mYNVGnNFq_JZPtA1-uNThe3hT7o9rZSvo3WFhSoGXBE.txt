Co-Planning Meeting Agenda: Criteria for Success
Schools that plan to use their own co-planning meeting agenda should use this rubric to evaluate whether their agenda contains the four required elements, as outlined in the Co-Planning for Differentiated Instruction practice (see p. 3.5). Specifically, these elements include:

1. ALIGN on the lessons' content: Discuss the upcoming week’s lesson content, including objectives, end-of-lesson assessments, criteria for success, and major lesson activities.
2. REFLECT on student data: Reviewing recent student data, identifying key strengths and needs in both trends and individual student needs
3. REMOVE barriers to access: Proactively designing instruction to remove barriers to access, such as multiple means of representation, scaffolding, flexible grouping, etc
4. PLAN for future, differentiated instruction: Determining what each teacher will do to effectively deliver the planned adaptations and accommodations in upcoming instruction

Schools can identify next steps to refine their co-planning meeting agenda to include these elements.
