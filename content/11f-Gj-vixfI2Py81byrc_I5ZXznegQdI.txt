Part 3. Guidance for Schools: Maintaining Fidelity Amidst Blackout
Weeks
Key Principle: Consider the length of your implementation cycles .Blackout weeks may occur
within cycles, but do not shorten or shift cycles.

Mitigation Strategies
1. Frontload and Backload: Protect before and after blackout weeks — guarantee 3 full
sessions those weeks.
2. Partial Weeks Still Count: For weeks with just 1 day off (holidays, PD), plan for 2–3
sessions instead of canceling.
3. Creative Scheduling: Use advisory, warm-ups, or shortened class time to preserve RR
dosage during disrupted weeks.

4. Don’t Merge Pauses and Blackouts: If a blackout lands near a pause week, keep the
pause week separate so there’s still a reset and data review.
5. Data Reflection: In pause weeks, analyze:
a. How did the blackout impact fidelity?
b. Were dosage adjustments enough?
c. What can we adjust in scheduling or staff support for the next cycle?
