A decision-making guide and tool designed for general and special educators to select effective
co-planning structures, utilize data, and maximize student benefits.

collaboration approach to the intended impact. Knowing what each model enables—speed,
depth, or strategic redesign—ensures you leverage the right format for maximum student
benefit.

B. Decision Scenarios
● Quick Fix (Quick Check-In) Scenario: An exit ticket shows one student
misunderstanding the question format. Teachers meet briefly to adjust tomorrow’s
prompt template.
● Weekly Sync (Weekly Co-Planning) Scenario: Quiz results over three days reveal that
the subgroup of students with IEPs scores 20% below their peers. Teachers schedule
the weekly block to plan targeted small-group scaffolds.
● Strategic Unit Review (Unit-Cycle Planning) Scenario: End-of-unit test indicates
persistent gaps in critical thinking skills across the class. Teachers convene a 90-minute
session to redesign the next unit’s assessments and embed new performance tasks.

C. Quick Decision-Making Checklist
1. For each row, place a check in the column matching your situation
2. Count checks in each column
3. Choose the model with 2 or more checks
4. If there is a tie or no column has 2 checks, move one level up (Quick → Weekly → Unit)

4. Agenda Snapshots
Quick Check-In (5 min):
● Identify: Single data point review
● Adjust: Agree on an immediate instructional tweak
● Confirm: Assign owner and timeline for follow-up

Weekly Co-Planning (45 min):
● Align (10 min): Review upcoming objectives, success criteria, and lesson activities
● Reflect (10 min): Examine weekly data trends; surface barriers for focal
students/subgroups
● Remove (15 min): Plan adaptations and scaffolds to address identified barriers
● Select (5 min): Choose a co-teaching model and assign roles for implementation
● Commit (5 min): Document next steps, owners, and schedule a brief check-in

Unit-Cycle Planning (60–120 min):
● Align: Map unit objectives, assessments, and criteria for success
● Reflect: Deep dive into cumulative and subgroup data to identify persistent gaps
● Remove: Design unit-level scaffolds, culturally responsive strategies, and
accommodations
● Select: Determine pacing, grouping structures, and co-teaching approaches across unit
● Commit: Finalize unit plan, assign responsibilities, and set dates for interim data
checkpoints
