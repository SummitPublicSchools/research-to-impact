Using AI to Create Fluency Passages
A Step-by-Step Guide
This tool guides educators in using AI to generate customized reading fluency passages that align with
grade‐level content, making fluency practice quick, sustainable and relevant for students.

Step 1. Define Your Parameters

Before you prompt ChatGPT, get clear on what you need to drive efficiency.
Choose the precise concept or skill (e.g., “net force in physics,” “Common Core A.CED.A.1:
creating equations,” “Reconstruction era political changes”)
Decide on a target word‐count (e.g., 200 words) and whether you want text only

Specify the grade level or Lexile band (e.g., “9th‐grade reading level,” “600L–800L”).
Why this matters: The clearer your instructions, the more likely the first draft needs minimal adjusting.

Step 2. Draft & Vet Your Fluency Passage

Generate your passage and immediately check its suitability before moving on.
Ask ChatGPT the passage based on your parameters: “Write a 200-word text-only fluency
passage on net force at a 9th‐grade reading level.”

If there are mistakes, ask ChatGPT to fix them.
Lesson learned: Early vetting ensures accuracy and saves you time from reprompting and reprinting.

Step 3. Produce & Archive Your Resource

Turn your vetted passage into a polished, reusable resource.
Copy and paste the passage into the Oral Reading Fluency Passage Generator or the Repeated
Reading Text Generator. Fill in the title, author/teacher, date, etc.
Download as a PDF, print, and use.
Time Saving Tip: Save PDFs. A well‐organized folder means no duplicate work next semester.

Step 4. Develop Your DOK Question Bank

Quickly generate and refine questions that span all depths of knowledge.
Prompt ChatGPT: “Create eight questions on this passage, two at each DOK Level (1-4), with
answers.”
Ensure each question targets an important term or idea from the passage.
If a question seems too simple or challenging, ask ChatGPT to rephrase or deepen it.
Purposeful Planning: Creating a question and answer bank supports prep and planning for teachers.
