Establishing Daily Task Delegation (Example & Template)
This document lists a wide variety of tasks that will need to be completed throughout the average week, along with an example of delegation, support, and notes for executing each task. Co-teaching teams should adapt this template for their specific context and should complete it as a team, delegating roles and responsibilities as they see fit. Teams may also choose to rotate or re-assign tasks throughout the year.
