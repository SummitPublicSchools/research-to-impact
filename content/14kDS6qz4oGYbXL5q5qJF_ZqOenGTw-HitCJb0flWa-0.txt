FOCAL POPULATION COLLABORATION MAPPING TOOL
Reading the Signals
Mapping the adults who serve your focal population

Use this tool to map how the adults who serve your focal population are — or aren't yet — collaborating around the work of teaching them. The questions are designed to surface what's working, what's missing, and where a small shift could change the conditions for students.

SPACE FOR YOUR THINKING
1. FOCAL POPULATION
Which subgroup of students are you focusing on? Why this group, specifically?

2. WHO SERVES THESE STUDENTS
Who are the educators who primarily serve and educate these students? Think both about:
* Specialists or case managers who hold formal responsibility for this group
* The general educators in whose classrooms these students spend most of their day

3. CURRENT COLLABORATION
Are there formal structures for these educators to collaborate for our focal population? If yes, how often and for how much time? Specifically, consider whether there are formal structures or routines to:
* Look at student data together
* Align instructional goals and priorities
* Plan lessons together

4. REALITY ON THE GROUND
Are the structures you named actually happening? Are they consistent? Productive?
* If yes: what's making them work?
* If not: what's getting in the way?

ACTION PLANNING:
Based on your mapping and reflection, what is one lever you could shift — or one bright spot you could expand — that would meaningfully change the experience and outcomes for your focal population?
