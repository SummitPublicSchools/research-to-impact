Co-Planning Agenda Templates
This document contains co-planning agenda templates and accompanying one-page “placemats” that lower cognitive load for collaborating teachers, clearly separating guidance from note-taking, and offering both Basic and Guided versions. All designs draw on the four key components (Align, Reflect, Remove, Select) bookended by Connect and Commit steps.

Use the links below to find specific templates and placemats. Make a copy, and use as needed.
1. Co-Planning Agenda: Lean Version
A lean template for experienced pairs who need minimal prompts and maximum note space.
Go to Lean Agenda.

2. Co-Planning Agenda: Guided Version
A more scaffolded template for newer collaborators, with each step framed by guiding questions and dedicated note areas.
Go to Guided Agenda.
3. Co-Planning Quick Reference
Laminate this and keep it at your desk or whiteboard for a fast visual of each step.
Go to Quick Reference.
4. Roles & Reminders Placemats
Laminate and keep available for a reminder of roles and responsibilities.
Go to Roles & Reminders.

This tool was developed by Marshall CoLab and reflects adaptations and innovations from the field. It is part of the growing Research to Impact Practice toolkit, a set of tools developed as IGNITE Network resources, to advance outcomes for historically underserved students.

Co-Planning Quick Reference

PRE-WORK
– Compile and prepare needed data, materials, and lesson plans
1. CONNECT & SET (5 min)
– Revisit the previous meeting’s goals
– Define today’s key priorities / focus
2. ALIGN (10 min)
– What are the lesson objectives and core content components?
– How will we know students succeeded?
3. REFLECT (10 min)
– What does our most recent student data show?
– Which students need extra support?
4. REMOVE (10 min)
– What barriers surfaced? What others do we anticipate based on student learning needs and profiles?
– What adaptations or accommodations address them?
5. SELECT (7 min)
– How will we deliver instruction, and in what format?
– Who does what, with whom, when?
6. COMMIT (3 min)
– Define and capture next steps and owners
