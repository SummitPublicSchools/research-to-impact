How to Measure
When implementing routine data cycles, monitoring how useful, usable, and utilized the practice is
by team members and across the school system can accelerate learning to improve outcomes for
students. Be aware that improved process measures are usually necessary to ensure a high enough
level of fidelity before outcomes will see improvement. At minimum, these measures are most essential:
