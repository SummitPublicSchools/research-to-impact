Sentence Frames for Peer Corrections
(Day 2 Partner Reading)

Use these sentence frames to provide feedback to the reader when they make an error. After
every correction, the reader goes back and reads the whole sentence again.

1. Wrong Word
● “The word is ___. Can you try the sentence again?”
● “Oops, that word is ___. Let’s read the sentence one more time.”
● “Try that word again: ___. Then reread the whole sentence.”

2. Stuck on a Word (after ~3 seconds)
● “That word is ___. Can you say it with me?”
● “The word is ___. Now try the whole sentence again.”
● “That one is hard. It’s ___. You try it, then read the sentence again.”

3. Skipped Word

“You skipped a word here. Try the sentence again and include it.”
“There’s a word missing. Let’s go back and read the sentence together.”
“You missed this word: ___.”
“One word got left out. Read the sentence again and catch it.”

4. Added Word
● “I heard an extra word. Let’s read it again without the extra word.”
● “You added a word. Try the sentence again the way it’s written.”

5. Mispronounced part of a word
● “Close. The word is ___. Say it with me, then reread the sentence.”
● “Good try. Break it into parts: ___. Now read the sentence again.”

6. Self-corrected a word on their own
● “Nice catch. You fixed that one yourself. Keep going.”
● “You’ve got it. Read on from ___.”

7. Reading too fast or without pausing (rate and prosody)
● “Slow down a little so every word is clear.”
● “Pause at the period, then keep reading.”
● “Try that sentence again with expression.”
This tool was developed by Stephanie Lassalle of Marshall Street and Glynis Shulters of Layered Education.
It reflects adaptations and innovations from the field and is part of the Research to Impact Practice toolkit, a set
of tools developed as IGNITE Network resources, to advance outcomes for historically underserved students.
