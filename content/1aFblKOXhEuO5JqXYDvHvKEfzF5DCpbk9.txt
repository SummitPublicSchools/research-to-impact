Increasing Effective Co-Planning:
Guiding Prompts for General Education Teachers
Practitioner Challenge Observed: Newer special education teachers paired with experienced general education colleagues
at times report feeling less prepared to execute their roles in co-planning and may rely heavily on their partner to guide the
meeting. This can limit their growth as collaborative instructional partners and be a barrier to growth and scale of the practice.
Change Idea: Train and position general educators to support and guide their special education team members to effectively
engage in, and where appropriate, facilitate co-planning meetings.
Working Theory: If general education teachers use simple, structured prompts during co-planning with special education
partners—and receive training on how to use them—we can build special educators’ skills to lead future co-planning,
strengthening collaboration and improving outcomes for teachers and students.

PROMPTS TO TRY
Steering Question:
Here’s the specific content we’re doing this week. Are there any concepts that you want more clarity on?

Align on
Content

Guiding Prompt:
● Share what you plan to teach in the upcoming lesson / unit with your special education teacher.
● Ask them to state what questions they have about the content.
● Prompt them to ask for any clarification or support they need on the content or how to teach it.
Steering Question:
Here is my most recent data. Some of the trends I’m noticing are X and Y. Is there anything else that
stands out to you that we should discuss?

Reflect on
Student Data

Remove
Barriers to
Access

Guiding Prompt:
Now is the part of the meeting where you want to have us pull up data and review it together. We want to
look for trends of what is going well and what has not yet been mastered, as well as who may need more
support. Do you want to guide me through that?
Steering Question:
X student has been getting stuck on Y part of the work. Based on your knowledge of student
needs/abilities/accommodations, do you have a suggestion for what we could do to get them unstuck?
Guiding Prompt:
Now that we know what students we’re focusing on, could you guide me through removing barriers based
on the learning profiles of our students so that we can increase their access to the material?
Steering Question:
With all of this in mind, what will each of us do next to provide instructional support to our students?

Plan for
Future
Instruction

Guiding Prompt:
This is where we each consider our next steps for instruction – what you’ll do in Learning Center to
support student progress, and how I can strategically use small groups or other instructional delivery
methods to meet the needs of various subgroups and individual students. Where do you think we should
start?

This tool was developed by Marshall CoLab in collaboration with educators at Summit Public Schools. It reflects

developed as IGNITE Network resources, to advance outcomes for historically underserved students.
