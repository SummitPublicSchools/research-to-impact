Co-Planning Enabling Conditions: Self-Assessment Tool for Leaders
This self-assessment helps school leaders identify which conditions for co-planning are in place and which to strengthen next. It is a companion to Co-Planning Enabling Conditions: A Guide for Leaders and the Co-Planning practice in the Marshall Research-to-Impact Practices. Complete it with your leadership team before launch and revisit it midyear.
