Capture evidence of strong co-planning and identify where to build skill.
Directions:
Record what you observe in each component, then name a coaching priority. Where something isn’t in place yet, treat it as the next place to build skill.

Align
* Both teachers share the same objectives and success criteria
* Special educator engages with the content and raises clarifying questions

Reflect
* Analysis identifies error types and specific students, not just scores
* Outcome goals are set, especially for students with disabilities

Remove
* Barriers and misconceptions are addressed with specific scaffolds, accommodations, or specially designed instruction
* Special educator’s expertise leads the differentiation

Plan
* Co-teaching model fits the purpose of the lesson
* Roles and next steps are specific, owned, and recorded

Common Stall Points
* Pre-work incomplete, plans or data not ready
* Meeting does not complete all four components
* Time drifts to logistics or compliance
* Planning is one-sided or narrows to a single student
