Fluency Feedback Sentence Stems
Guidance for giving positive and coaching feedback to a peer
Glow
* “Your speed was strong when ... “
* “Your expression was interesting when ... “
* “I really like how you ... “
* “You did a great job at ... “
* “It was clear when you ... “
Grow
* “You can adjust your speed when ... “
* “You can add more expression when ... “
* “You missed the word ... “
* “You may want to consider ... “
* “Next time try ... “
* “It was unclear to me when you ... “
* “It may be more useful to ... “
This tool was developed by educators at Collegiate Academies’ G.W. Carver High School and adapted by Marshall CoLab for external use. It reflects adaptations and innovations from the field and is part of the growing Research to Impact Practice toolkit, developed as part of the IGNITE Network resources, to advance outcomes for historically underserved students.
