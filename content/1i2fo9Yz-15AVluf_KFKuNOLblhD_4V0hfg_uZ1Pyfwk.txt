Strong routine data cycles run on clear routines. Teams that set their time, roles and norms up front spend their time on analysis and planning, not logistics. Use this tool with your coach to assess readiness, plan logistics, define roles, agree on norms, and reflect on your practice throughout the year.
When to use it: Complete Sections 1–4 when you launch co-planning. Revisit Section 5 each quarter. Returning teams can start with Section 5.
