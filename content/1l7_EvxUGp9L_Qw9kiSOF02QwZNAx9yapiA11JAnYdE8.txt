Leverage this resource to turn your vision into a concrete plan for who will co-plan, how, and with what support.
Your vision tells you where you're headed; your launch plan maps how you'll get there. Capture your design decisions for who will co-plan, in what format, and with what support — then set milestones for your launch. Focus on developing a thoughtful start you can build from, not a perfect plan.
Directions: Work through the three parts below to design your teams, decide your logistics, prepare your teams to launch, and set your first milestones. If you completed the Vision Setting and Enabling Conditions tools, keep them in view. Your vision is the anchor, and your priority conditions shape what you build first.
If co-planning already exists at your school, treat this as your plan to relaunch or strengthen the practice — the same decisions apply.
