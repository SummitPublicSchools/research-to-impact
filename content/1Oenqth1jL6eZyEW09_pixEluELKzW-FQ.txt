Co-Planning Look-Fors & Common Pitfalls
A coaching reference for observing and strengthening each part of the meeting.
Use this reference alongside the Co-Planning Observation Tool to sharpen what you look for and to
diagnose where a team needs support. Each component shows strong practice, where it commonly
breaks down, and a coaching move to build skill.

The Co-Planning Meeting at a Glance
● Pre-work: role-specific preparation completed before the meeting
● Connect and set: review norms and set intentions (≈5 min)
● Align: objectives, assessment, success criteria, key activities (≈10 min)
● Reflect: review recent data and set outcome goals (≈10 min)
● Remove: design supports that address identified barriers (≈10 min)
● Plan: decide delivery, roles, and co-teaching model (≈7 min)

Pre-Work Expectations
● General educator: shares the lesson plan in advance; completes the exit ticket as a student
would; brings recent scored work and data
● Special educator: annotates the lesson for content and access barriers; completes the exit
ticket; brings data from specialized instruction and clarifying questions
