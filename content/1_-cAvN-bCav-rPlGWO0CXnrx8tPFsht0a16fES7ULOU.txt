Routine Data Cycle Template Protocol
This protocol offers a simple template for educators implementing routine data cycles. It builds on the IGNITE Network's learning with school teams and reflects adaptations from the field.
COLLECT DATA
Before you analyze, gather student work to use as the data source for this cycle. Consider entering results in a simple data collection template.

Data Source:
* Exit Slip/Cool Down
* Homework
* Classwork Activity
* Summative Assessment
* Writing Assignment

* Other (Specify)

ANALYZE DATA TO IDENTIFY STUDENT NEEDS
Analyze students’ work to identify strengths and challenges. Then, examine root causes and areas for focused support. Finally, set goals to measure progress.
STEPS
NOTES
1. Identify Strengths
* What skills are students demonstrating successfully?
* What evidence supports this?

2. Identify Challenges
* What skills or concepts are students struggling with?
* What evidence supports this?

3. Consider Causes
* What misconceptions or gaps are evident?
* What instructional factors may have contributed?

4. Identify priority focus area(s)
* What is the highest-leverage focus area(s) for improving student learning before the next assessment?

5. Create a SMART goal that will move the needle for students
* What evidence supports progress towards this goal?
By [date], proficiency on ____ will increase from ___% to ___%, as measured by __________.
