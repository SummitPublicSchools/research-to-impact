Designing Co-Planning Routines: A Team Planning Tool
Strong co-planning runs on clear routines. Teams that set their time, roles and norms up front spend their meetings on instruction, not logistics. Use this tool with your coach to assess readiness, plan logistics, define roles, agree on norms, and reflect on your practice throughout the year.
When to use it: Complete Sections 1–5 when you launch co-planning. Revisit Section 6 each quarter. Returning teams can start with Section 6.

SECTION 3: CO-PLANNING LOGISTICS
Recommended: co-plan at least biweekly for 30–45 minutes. Co-teaching teams meet at least weekly.

SECTION 6: REFLECTING ON CURRENT PRACTICES
Returning teams: start here at the beginning of the year. All teams: revisit each quarter. Use these questions to discuss what’s working well, what needs improvement, and how you will shift your co-planning logistics, roles and ways of working in the coming quarter.
