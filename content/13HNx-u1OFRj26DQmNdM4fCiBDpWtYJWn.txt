Modeling Fluency Instruction in PD:
Strengthening Educator Skill and Confidence
This resource provides school leaders, instructional coaches, and teacher leaders with guidance
for building educator skill and confidence in implementing oral reading fluency routines,
particularly in secondary settings. The accompanying video demonstration (see below)
illustrates how to model the practice during professional learning sessions.

Key Considerations for Modeling Repeated Reading in Staff Development

Begin with the
purpose and
rationale

● Anchor learning in the why: reading fluency (accuracy, rate, and
prosody) is essential for comprehension
● Reinforce that fluency practice is every educator’s responsibility
● Connect repeated reading to broader school literacy goals,
inclusion, and equitable access to grade-level content
● Emphasize that structured, short, and consistent routines can
produce meaningful growth for students

Model the
routine in full

● Demonstrate the complete protocol exactly as students will
experience it
○ Timed cold and warm reads
○ Feedback and error practice
○ Track rate, accuracy, and growth
● Use actual classroom materials (i.e., timers, calculators, sample
passages, progress graphs)
● Narrate what makes the model effective
● Invite staff to rotate through roles (readers, peer coach, observer)

Make learning
interactive and
reflective

● After the modeled routine, facilitate a quick debrief
○ What felt easy/clear or challenging/confusing?
○ How did the feedback process feel as both a giver and
receiver?
○ What might this experience feel like for students?
● Encourage teachers to connect insights to their instructional
planning
● Capture reflections on what strong facilitation looks, sounds, and
feels like
