Common Barriers and Pitfalls
in Repeated Reading
Every team hits barriers to successful repeated reading implementation. Those barriers shift as
implementation deepens, from mindsets and buy-in to skill development and consistency.
Browse the General Guidance section for broad direction, or use the Common Barriers table
below to match a specific barrier to a strategy.

Unable to deliver the routine
consistently due to a lack of
protected time

Use the Recommended Time Allocation section to
advocate for fluency block integration (p. 4.9)
Build a schedule to plan the frequency and
expectations (e.g., 3x/weekly) for the delivery of the
intervention
Use planning calendars to account for short weeks or
testing interruptions (p. 4.8)

Need more strategies for
boosting student motivation

Use goal setting, progress tracking, and fluency
graphs to boost ownership (p. 4.18)
Use progress visuals (cold vs. hot reads) to celebrate
growth with students, and where appropriate, families
Leverage motivational framing and student
conferencing as outlined in the engagement section
(p. 4.13)

Lack of knowledge on how to
adapt the routine for different
students

Use student data to determine which variation of the
practice is most appropriate (peer vs. teacher-led) (p.
4.10)
Adjust the number of rereads, support tools, and
pacing based on student need (p. 4.12)
Provide visual tracking aids and adjust passage length
if needed (p. 4.19)

Difficulty tracking or using
fluency data to inform
instruction

Use data trackers or simple bar graphs with students,
such as the progress monitoring graph for cold/hot
reads (p. 4.18)
Schedule fluency data huddles every 6–8 weeks to
analyze trends (p. 4.21)

Lack of appropriate materials
or passages

Use Intervention Central’s Reading Fluency Passage
Generator to convert grade-level texts into fluency
passages (p. 4.12)
Select short excerpts from existing curriculum
(200–350 words) (p. 4.12)
Use AI tools to simplify or adapt content-based texts
while maintaining rigor - review Leverage AI to build
fluency passages
