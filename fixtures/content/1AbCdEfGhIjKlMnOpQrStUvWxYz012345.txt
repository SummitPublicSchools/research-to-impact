Enabling Conditions Guide (fixture sample text)

This sample page stands in for the text of a real guide. Before a school launches co-planning, leaders protect a weekly forty-five minute block in the master schedule so that every general educator and education specialist pair can meet.

Schools that skip this step usually report that planning time is the first thing to disappear when the bell schedule changes.

Rate each condition from one to four. A rating of one means the condition is absent; a rating of four means it is routine and survives staff turnover. Bring the completed self-assessment to the leadership huddle and choose a single condition to strengthen this quarter.
